# MeitY Implementation Roadmap and Corrigenda

**MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (MeitY)**  
*Notifications, Corrigenda, and Statutory Enforcement Schedules*  

---

## 1. Statutory Background and Notification Framework

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 was passed by the Parliament and received Presidential assent on August 11, 2023. Under Section 1(2) of the Act, different provisions come into force on different dates as appointed by the Central Government via Official Gazette notifications.

---

## 2. Phased Enforcement Timeline

### Phase I: Institutional Setup (Months 0–6)
* **Effective Date:** 1st November 2023
* **Provisions in Force:**
  - Section 1: Short title and commencement.
  - Section 2: Definitions.
  - Section 18 to 26: Constitution and establishment of the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI), appointment of Chairperson, Members, and administrative structure.
  - Section 40: Protection of action taken in good faith.
  - Section 41 to 43: Rule-making powers and removal of difficulties.
* **Core Mandate:** Establish the digital office of the Board, formulate internal procedural guidelines, and operationalize digital infrastructure.

### Phase II: Core Fiduciary Obligations and Principal Rights (Months 6–18)
* **Effective Date:** 1st December 2024
* **Provisions in Force:**
  - Section 4: Grounds for processing personal data.
  - Section 5: Notice in 22 Eighth Schedule languages.
  - Section 6: Consent architecture and registration framework for Consent Managers.
  - Section 7: Certain legitimate uses.
  - Section 8: General obligations of Data Fiduciaries (except sub-section 7 erasure timelines).
  - Section 11 to 15: Rights and duties of Data Principals (access, correction, grievance redressal, nomination).
  - Section 27 to 34: Adjudication mechanisms, inquiry powers of the Board, penalties, and appeals to TDSAT.
* **Core Mandate:** All commercial organizations operating as Data Fiduciaries must update privacy notices, obtain explicit affirmative consent, and establish grievance redressal channels.

### Phase III: Significant Data Fiduciaries, Children Protections, and Penalties (Months 18–24)
* **Effective Date:** 1st June 2025
* **Provisions in Force:**
  - Section 9: Strict protections regarding personal data of children and prohibition of tracking/behavioral advertising.
  - Section 10: Additional obligations for Significant Data Fiduciaries (mandatory Indian resident DPO, independent annual data audits, and DPIAs).
  - Section 16: Operationalization of the negative list for cross-border data transfers.
  - The Schedule: Full operationalization of statutory penalties up to ₹250 crore.
* **Core Mandate:** Full statutory enforcement across enterprise platforms, hyperscalers, and Significant Data Fiduciaries.

## 3. Official Gazette Corrigenda

### Corrigendum 1 (G.S.R. 612(E) dated 18th August 2023)
* **In Section 12(1):** The term *"or erasure"* was clarified to confirm that right to erasure is governed by Section 12(3) without prejudice to mandatory statutory retention requirements under banking, tax, and anti-money laundering laws.
* **In Section 18(2):** Clarified the legal character of the Board as a body corporate having perpetual succession and common seal.

### Corrigendum 2 (G.S.R. 784(E) dated 24th November 2023)
* **In Section 17(1):** Clarified that the exemption for enforcement of any legal right or claim does not absolve any Data Fiduciary from the reasonable security safeguards required under Section 8(5).
* **In Section 6(3):** Reaffirmed that while notices and consent requests must offer 22 languages, digital platforms may utilize automated translation modules certified by MeitY-recognized linguistic toolkits.

---

## 4. Key Statutory Distinction: "Enacted" vs. "In Force"

* **Enactment Date:** 11th August 2023 (Date of Presidential assent).
* **Current Status:** Provisions operate according to the staggered gazette notifications outlined in Sections 1(2).
* **Legal Invariant:** A provision is statutory law upon enactment, but compliance obligations and penalty enforcement only attach once the specific section is appointed by gazette notification.
